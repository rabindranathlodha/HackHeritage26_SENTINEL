MOTION SPECS
Screen change
420ms · cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1) · 12px upward drift + fade. Slow enough to feel accompanied, never snappy.
Question to question
320ms crossfade, outgoing question leaves first (80ms stagger). Progress bar fills over 500ms ease-out.
Ambient (Home only)
One radial ember glow, 12s breathe loop, scale 1→1.14, opacity .55→.8. Below the fold of attention.
Sync
2.4s slow rotation, no spinner burst. Completion is a fade to a line of text, never a checkmark pop.
Reduced motion
All of the above degrade to instant. Nothing in the product depends on motion to be understood.

08

Words we use, words we never use

WE SAY
wellness check · how you're doing
support · your welfare team
things have felt heavier
we're here whenever you need a moment
stays on your phone
WE NEVER SAY
depression, anxiety, disorder, risk
score, band, rating, index
you missed last week
keep your streak going
assessment, screening, monitoring
NON-NEGOTIABLES
No number, score or band is ever shown to the person.
Consent toggles ship OFF and reverse in one tap.
Nav sits in the same three places on every screen.
Every action works with no signal.
Text ≥15px, targets ≥56px, contrast ≥4.5:1, one-handed at 360px.
